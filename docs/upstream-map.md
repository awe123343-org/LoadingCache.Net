## Evidence and maintenance obligations

The initial local policy suite had 26 cases per runtime and 120 simulator runs; TimerWheel had 18 cases per runtime, including a corrected tail-deschedule counterexample. Those are historical module checks, not qualification of later runtime changes. Later evidence is linked from [release readiness](release-readiness.md) and [benchmark methodology](benchmark-methodology.md). Public Caffeine benchmarks do not constitute an upstream test-suite run.

For every actual adaptation record: local path, upstream repository/tag/full SHA/path/URL, applicable licence, retained attribution, modified sections and rationale, relevant upstream tests, local tests/evidence and outstanding review. This applies to tests, traces and samples as well as production code. No upstream endorsement or completed legal review is claimed.
