# V1 performance and release gates

Adopted on 16 September 2026: stop general resident-hit micro-tuning and qualify the retained candidate. Caffeine/MemoryCache remain public comparisons, but winning every cell is not a release requirement. Reopen optimisation for a concrete correctness/resource failure or a reproducible breach of the fixed budgets below. Full feature and race contracts remain unchanged.

The [retained MemoryCache matrix](benchmarks/parallel-resident-put-20260916/README.md) contains 584 processes/2,920 measured samples: of 100 main configurations, 68 had lower cost in both rounds, 27 higher and five mixed. This is not traffic-weighted success. Single-reader preboxed stats-off/on pooled medians span roughly 25–44 ns without expiry, 44–55 ns TTL and 61–74 ns TTI/TTL+TTI, versus MemoryCache 23–26 ns on that workstation. These are aggregate wall costs, not request p99 or process CPU.

## Fixed acceptance budgets

These are project decisions, not upstream standards. Fix configuration and budgets before measurement; never move a threshold after seeing a failure.

1. **Correctness:** no known unresolved contract/race defect; deterministic regressions pass. Invalidation fencing, hard freshness and waiter cancellation cannot be weakened for speed.
2. **Self-regression:** compare frozen candidate profiles for resident read, mixed mutation, capacity pressure and async loading, stats OFF/OFF and ON/ON on actual .NET 8/10. A reproducible cost/CPU/throughput regression above 10%, beyond A/A noise, blocks the candidate until fixed or its necessary trade-off explicitly reviewed. Start with three reverse paired rounds; unstable results are Inconclusive, not an invitation to rerun until green. This bounds regression, not initial adequacy.
3. **Resident service:** freeze a representative public host's payload, operations/request, expiry, hit/miss mix, backend time and arrival rate. The initial budget is ≤5% incremental whole-process CPU and request p99 against a matched preloaded-value control. CPU is `(CPU_cache−CPU_control)/CPU_control` for equal completed work, including maintenance/final drain; also report CPU-seconds/request and offered/completed/failed counts. Preserve response/business work without adding unrelated work to dilute cache cost. Latency includes queue delay. Insufficient A/A precision yields Inconclusive, not Passed or a proven regression. The [bounded diagnostic](v1-service-precision-diagnostic-20260918.md) leaves this gate Inconclusive; no budget was changed.
4. **Miss/burst:** use identical backend/resource configuration for coalescing/expiry/refresh. Same-generation invocation bounds are exact contracts, not percentage tolerances. Report expected rejection/timeout as outcomes, not just successful p99. Normal load must not accumulate backlog; burst recovery must meet its predeclared budget. Separate native MemoryCache's missing coalescing from equal-work performance.
5. **Allocation/retention:** warmed common hits target zero incremental B/op with disclosed harness cost. Miss/write/callback allocation, background GC and retained bytes are separate measurements. Check resident/weight/flight/notification bounds and explain post-clear/disposal roots; a near-zero mean or one GC assertion is insufficient.
6. **Stability:** run existing representative combinations for at least 30 minutes each on both runtimes, plus one continuous ≥8-hour fixed-capacity churn/clear/dispose endurance. Do not resume or add interrupted durations. Preserve failures, diagnose and rerun only affected evidence. [Current explicit-limit macOS results](v1-stability-results-20260918.md) passed; they do not prove all schedules/platforms or unlimited-default retention.
7. **Policy and release:** retain fixed-trace miss/backend/residency accounting without hidden capacity, reduced replay or weaker freshness. Review API/behaviour, examples, real packages, Source Link/symbols, provenance/security and claimed platforms. Managed prerelease and AOT qualification are distinct routes; stable readiness remains in the [release ledger](release-readiness.md).

There is no production traffic/SLO proving the initial 5% budget universally appropriate. Different ultra-low-latency workloads need their own agreed CPU/tail budgets. Further reductions below accepted costs and bounded metadata improvements can follow in 1.x, provided TTL, cancellation, ownership and callback compatibility remain intact.

## Basis and measurement limits

Caffeine separates [throughput](https://github.com/ben-manes/caffeine/wiki/Benchmarks) from [policy efficiency](https://github.com/ben-manes/caffeine/wiki/Efficiency); historical published numbers are not current same-machine evidence. [MemoryCache](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/aspnet/core/performance/caching/memory?view=aspnetcore-10.0) has different population, size and cleanup contracts; match statistics explicitly without assuming equal counter coverage.

Use [BenchmarkDotNet good practices](https://benchmarkdotnet.org/articles/guides/good-practices.html), distinguish [allocation diagnosis](https://benchmarkdotnet.org/articles/configs/diagnosers.html) from live retention and [microbenchmark statistics](https://benchmarkdotnet.org/articles/features/statistics.html) from request tails. [SRE guidance](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/) supports outcome-based budgets; [SemVer](https://semver.org/) and [library compatibility](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/core/compatibility/library-change-rules) do not require beating every competitor.

Estimate added cores from actual CPU ns/op × operations/s / 1e9, never multi-worker wall ns/op. Allocation rate is B/op × operations/s, checked against GC/tails. Extra misses must be valued by real backend work, not an unweighted average hit ratio. These conversions are planning arithmetic, not measured results or release thresholds.
